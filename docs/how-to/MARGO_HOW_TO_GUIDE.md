## 1. Create an account with email (Tier 1 essential)

**Who:** Member  
**Goal:** Start a Margo account with email and password.

### Before you start
- A working email inbox you can open on this device.
- A password with at least 10 characters, including a letter and a number.

### Steps
1. Open Margo and tap **Sign In** on the **You** tab (mobile) or **Sign In** in the top nav (desktop).
2. On **Create account**, enter your **Email**.
3. Enter your **Password** (hint on screen: **10+ chars, letter & number**).
4. Check **I have read and agree to the** **Terms of Service** and **Privacy Policy**.
5. Tap **Create account**.

### What you should see when it worked
- **Check email** with **Link sent to** your address and a link to **sign in**.
- After you confirm (see how-to #4), **Sign in** works and onboarding may send you to **Welcome**.

### If something goes wrong
- **Accept to continue.** or **Please review and accept our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy to continue.** — Check the consent box before **Create account**.
- **Use at least 10 characters.** / **Add at least one letter.** / **Add at least one number.** — Fix the password to match the hint.
- **That email already has an account.** — Tap **Sign in** and use **Forgot password?** if needed.

### Poster idea
**Headline:** Make an account with your email on Margo.  
**Line:** Agree to Terms, pick a strong password, and confirm from your inbox.

### Video idea (15–30 s)
1. **Sign In** screen → on-screen: **New here? Create account**
2. **Create account** form → **Email** and **Password**
3. Consent + **Create account** → **Check email**
4. Final frame → **Open the link, then sign in**

## 2. Create an account with Google (Tier 1 essential)

**Who:** Member  
**Goal:** Sign up with Google OAuth.

### Before you start
- A Google account.
- Browser allows pop-ups or redirects for Google sign-in.

### Steps
1. Tap **Sign In** ( **You** tab or top nav).
2. On **Create account** (or **Sign in** if you already use Google elsewhere), tap **Continue with Google**.
3. Complete Google’s sign-in flow in the browser window.
4. If Margo shows **Finish account**, check **Terms of Service** / **Privacy Policy** and tap **Continue**.
5. Complete **Welcome** if you are sent to `/welcome`.

### What you should see when it worked
- Toast **Welcome to Margo.** or you land on **Feed** / **Welcome** step **Your profile**.
- In **Account Settings** → **Security**, copy may say **You use Google or Discord sign-in. Change your password there.**

### If something goes wrong
- **Sign-in interrupted. Try again.** — Retry **Continue with Google**.
- **Please review and accept our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy to continue.** — Finish the **Finish account** step.
- **Something went wrong.** — Try again or use email signup (how-to #1).

### Poster idea
**Headline:** Join Margo with Google in one tap.  
**Line:** Continue with Google, accept Terms if asked, then set up your profile.

### Video idea
1. **Continue with Google** button
2. Google chooser (blur personal info)
3. **Finish account** if it appears → **Continue**
4. **Welcome** step 1 **Your profile**

## 7. Complete welcome onboarding (Tier 1 essential)

**Who:** Member  
**Goal:** Set name, username, photos, vibes, and role across four steps.

### Before you start
- Signed in; URL `/welcome?step=1` (or enforcer sends you here).

### Steps
1. **Step 1 of 4 — Your profile:** Enter **Name** and **Username** (with `@`). Tap **Next** when **@{username} is available**.
2. **Step 2 of 4 — Add your photos:** Optional **Add cover** / **Add profile photo**. Tap **Next** or **Skip for now**.
3. **Step 3 of 4 — Pick your vibe:** Choose at least 3 vibes (e.g. **Chill**, **Hope**, **Love**). Under **I'm here as**, pick **Listener** or **Artist**. Optional chips under **What best describes you? (optional)**. Tap **Next** (or **Skip for now** if vibes incomplete).
4. **Step 4 of 4 — You're in:** Read cards, then tap **Go to Feed**.

### What you should see when it worked
- **Feed** opens; your portrait may appear on **You**.
- **Saved** flash may appear briefly when steps save.

### If something goes wrong
- **Pick a personal username.** / **Not available.** / **Use at least 3 characters.** — Change **Username**.
- **Enter your name.**
- **Pick at least 3, or skip.** on vibes — Add vibes or **Skip for now**.
- **Couldn't save. Try again.** or **Couldn't upload. Try again.**

### Poster idea
**Headline:** Four quick steps to set up your Margo profile.  
**Line:** Name, photo, three vibes, and Listener or Artist.

### Video idea
1. **Your profile** → name + username
2. **Add your photos** → skip or add
3. **Pick your vibe** + **Listener**
4. **You're in** → **Go to Feed**
